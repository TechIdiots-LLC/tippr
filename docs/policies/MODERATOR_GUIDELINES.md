# Tippr Moderator Guidelines

*Effective Date: January 22, 2026*

*Last Revised: January 22, 2026*

Welcome to the Tippr Moderator Community! This document outlines the expectations, responsibilities, and best practices for moderators on Tippr.

---

## Welcome, Moderator!

Thank you for volunteering to help build and maintain our community. Moderators are the backbone of Tippr, ensuring that vaults remain welcoming, engaging, and safe for all users.

As a moderator, you are trusted with significant responsibilities. This guide will help you understand your role and provide resources to be an effective moderator.

---

## 1. Your Role as a Moderator

### 1.1 What Moderators Do

- **Enforce Rules**: Ensure content in your vault complies with vault rules and site-wide policies.
- **Curate Content**: Remove spam, off-topic content, and rule violations.
- **Foster Community**: Create an environment where users feel welcome to participate.
- **Respond to Reports**: Review user reports and take appropriate action.
- **Communicate**: Keep your community informed about rule changes and vault updates.

### 1.2 What Moderators Are NOT

- **Employees**: You are a volunteer, not an employee, contractor, or agent of TechIdiots LLC.
- **Paid Staff**: Moderation is a volunteer activity; there is no compensation.
- **Company Representatives**: You do not speak for or represent Tippr/TechIdiots LLC.

## 2. Volunteer Status & Legal Clarifications

### 2.1 Independent Volunteer Status

As stated in the [User Agreement](/help/useragreement), moderators are independent volunteers. You:

- Are not entitled to compensation or benefits;
- Cannot enter into agreements on behalf of Tippr;
- Act on your own judgment within site guidelines;
- May have your moderator status revoked at any time.

### 2.2 Liability

You are not personally liable for company actions, and the company is not liable for your personal actions as a moderator, provided you act within these guidelines.

### 2.3 Confidentiality

You may have access to user information (e.g., modmail, moderation logs). This information is confidential. Do not:

- Share modmail contents publicly;
- Disclose user information to third parties;
- Use user information for personal purposes.

## 3. Code of Conduct for Moderators

### 3.1 Act in Good Faith

- Make moderation decisions based on the best interests of your community.
- Apply rules consistently and fairly.
- Avoid personal bias in moderation decisions.

### 3.2 Be Transparent

- Provide clear rules for your vault.
- Explain moderation actions when possible.
- Be open to feedback from your community.

### 3.3 Respect Users

- Treat all users with dignity and respect.
- Avoid harassment, even when dealing with rule-breakers.
- Be patient with new users who may not understand the rules.

### 3.4 No Self-Dealing

- Do not moderate to benefit yourself financially.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Do not accept payment or gifts in exchange for moderation decisions.

### 3.5 Collaborate with Other Moderators

- Work constructively with your moderation team.
- Discuss significant decisions with fellow moderators.
- Support each other and share the workload.

## 4. Moderation Tools

### 4.1 Available Tools

- **Remove/Approve**: Remove rule-breaking content or approve flagged content.
- **Ban/Unban**: Temporarily or permanently ban users from your vault.
- **Mute**: Temporarily prevent users from messaging moderators.
- **Flair**: Assign user and post flair.
- **Wiki**: Manage vault wiki pages.
- **Modmail**: Communicate with users about moderation issues.
- **Moderation Log**: Review actions taken by all moderators.
- **AutoModerator**: Set up automated moderation rules.

### 4.2 Using Tools Responsibly

- Document significant moderation actions.
- Use bans proportionally—consider warnings first.
- Review AutoModerator rules regularly to avoid false positives.

## 5. Handling Content Violations

### 5.1 Vault Rule Violations

For content that breaks vault-specific rules:

1. Remove the content.
2. Consider warning the user (especially first-time offenses).
3. For repeated violations, consider temporary or permanent bans.

### 5.2 Site-Wide Policy Violations

For content that violates [Content Policy](/help/contentpolicy):

1. Remove the content immediately.
2. Report severe violations to site administrators.
3. Examples requiring admin attention:
   - Illegal content
   - Threats of violence
   - Personal information exposure (doxxing)
   - Child exploitation material

### 5.3 Appeals

- Consider user appeals in good faith.
- Provide clear explanations for decisions when asked.
- Be willing to reverse decisions if new information emerges.

## 6. Dealing with Difficult Situations

### 6.1 Harassment Campaigns

If your vault is targeted by a harassment campaign:

1. Use temporary restricted posting mode if needed.
2. Document the harassment.
3. Contact site administrators for assistance.

### 6.2 Burnout

Moderation can be stressful. To avoid burnout:

- Share responsibilities with your team.
- Take breaks when needed.
- Don't feel obligated to respond to everything immediately.
- Remember: you're a volunteer!

### 6.3 Disagreements with Users

- Stay calm and professional.
- Don't engage in arguments publicly.
- Take heated conversations to modmail.
- It's okay to step away and let another moderator handle the situation.

## 7. Building Your Community

### 7.1 Creating Good Rules

- Keep rules clear and concise.
- Explain the reasoning behind rules.
- Update rules as your community evolves.
- Pin rules in a visible location.

### 7.2 Encouraging Positive Participation

- Recognize quality contributors.
- Create regular discussion threads or events.
- Welcome new members.
- Lead by example with quality posts.

### 7.3 Growing Your Vault

- Cross-promote with related vaults.
- Engage with trending topics.
- Be patient—communities take time to grow.

## 8. Working with Site Administrators

### 8.1 When to Contact Admins

- Site-wide policy violations requiring action beyond your vault.
- Technical issues with moderation tools.
- Requests for features or support.

### 8.2 How to Contact Admins

- Use the "Contact Admins" feature.
- For urgent issues, email: modhelp@tippr.net
