# Tippr Content Policy

*Effective Date: January 22, 2026*

*Last Revised: January 22, 2026*

Tippr is a platform for communities to discuss, connect, and share. The following Content Policy governs what is allowed on Tippr and what is not.

---

## 1. Remember the Human

Tippr is a place for creating community and belonging, not for attacking marginalized or vulnerable groups. Everyone has a right to use Tippr free of harassment, bullying, and threats of violence.

## 2. Abide by Community Rules

In addition to this Content Policy, every community (vault) may have its own rules. Users are expected to follow both site-wide rules and vault-specific rules. Posting content that violates vault rules may result in removal of content or bans from that vault.

## 3. Respect the Privacy of Others

Posting personal information (doxxing) is prohibited. Do not post or share:

- Names, addresses, or other contact information
- Photographs of private individuals without consent
- Social security numbers, financial information, or passwords
- Any information that could lead to harassment or harm

## 4. Prohibited Content

The following content is not allowed on Tippr:

### 4.1 Illegal Content
Content that violates the law, including but not limited to:
- Content that sexualizes minors
- Content that facilitates illegal transactions
- Content that violates intellectual property rights

### 4.2 Harassment and Threats
- Threats of violence against individuals or groups
- Encouraging or inciting violence
- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Targeted harassment campaigns

### 4.3 Hateful Content
- Content that promotes hate based on identity or vulnerability
- Content designed to intimidate or silence others

### 4.4 Spam and Manipulation
- Spam, including repetitive or automated posting
- Vote manipulation
- Coordinated inauthentic behavior
- Fake engagement (likes, upvotes, awards)

### 4.5 Impersonation
- Impersonating individuals or entities in a misleading manner
- Creating accounts to mislead about your identity

### 4.6 Malicious Content
- Malware, phishing, or other harmful links
- Content designed to harvest personal information

## 5. Prohibited Behavior

### 5.1 Ban Evasion
Creating accounts to circumvent vault or site-wide bans is prohibited and may result in permanent suspension.

### 5.2 Brigading
Coordinating with others to overwhelm a vault or interfere with another community is prohibited.

### 5.3 Abuse of the Report System
Repeatedly filing false reports or abusing the reporting system is prohibited.

### 5.4 Breaking Tippr
Attempting to exploit vulnerabilities, disrupt services, or interfere with Tippr's operation is prohibited.

## 6. Moderator Responsibilities

Vault moderators are responsible for:

- Enforcing their vault's rules consistently
- Removing content that violates this Content Policy
- Acting in good faith when moderating

Moderators who fail to remove content that clearly violates site-wide rules may have their moderation privileges revoked.

## 7. Enforcement

### 7.1 Content Removal
Content that violates this policy may be removed without notice.

### 7.2 Account Suspension
Accounts that violate this policy may be temporarily or permanently suspended.

### 7.3 Vault Quarantine or Ban
Vaults that consistently host content violating this policy may be quarantined or banned.

### 7.4 Appeals
If you believe your content was removed or your account was suspended in error, you can appeal by contacting us at appeals@tippr.net.

## 8. Reporting Violations

If you see content that violates this policy:

1. Use the **Report** button on the content
2. Contact vault moderators through modmail
3. For serious violations, contact admins at contentpolicy@tippr.net

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Content Policy as needed. We will notify users of significant changes by posting an announcement.
