# Trophies

Trophies are small awards shown in the trophy case on a member's profile. They don't do anything; they mark things a member has done.

Some are given automatically, for example:

- **Verified email**: for confirming your email address (in your [preferences](/prefs/update/)). It goes away if you remove or change the address, until you verify the new one.
- **Gold**: for having [{{SITE_NAME}} gold](/gold).
- **1-Year Club, 2-Year Club, …**: on each anniversary of joining (you keep only the latest).

Others are given by the site's staff, for example to thank members who helped with the site or ran a community event. On a member's birthday on the site (the day they joined), a slice of cake shows beside their name.

If a trophy you should have is missing, [let the admins know](/contact#message-the-admins).
