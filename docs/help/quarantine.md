# Quarantined vaults

A quarantined vault is one whose content most people would find shocking or highly offensive, but which doesn't break the [content policy](/help/contentpolicy) outright. Rather than removing it, the site's staff quarantine it, so that only people who choose to see it do:

- Visiting it shows a warning first. To go in you choose to continue, which needs you to be logged in with a verified email address.
- Its posts don't show on the front page or in /v/all, and the vault doesn't show in vault search or recommendations.
- Its posts show no thumbnails, its stylesheet's images are blanked, it shows no ads, and its posts can't be given gold.

## For the vault's moderators

A quarantine is not a ban: the vault keeps working for people who opt in. If the vault changes, its moderators can ask the admins to review the quarantine through the [contact page](/contact#appeals), explaining what has changed.
