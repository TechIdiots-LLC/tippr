# Timeouts and suspensions

When an account breaks the [content policy](/help/contentpolicy) or the [user agreement](/help/useragreement), the site's staff can limit it. You'll get a message in your inbox saying what happened and why.

## Timeouts

An account in a timeout can still log in and read, but can't post, comment, vote or send messages, except to the admins. A timeout either lasts a set number of days, ending on its own, or lasts until the staff lift it. A banner at the top of the page says how long is left.

## Suspensions

A suspended account can't log in at all: its sessions end, and apps it authorized lose access. Suspensions are for serious or repeated problems, such as spam, harassment or evading an earlier timeout.

## Bans from a vault

A vault's moderators can ban you from that vault only: you can't post or comment there, but the rest of the site is unaffected. Ask the vault's moderators about it; the site's staff don't overrule moderators' decisions within their vaults except where a vault breaks the site's rules.

## Appeals

If you think a timeout or suspension was a mistake, [appeal it](/contact#appeals) from the contact page. During a timeout you can still message the admins; if you're suspended and can't log in, the contact page explains what to do. Please say which account, and why you think the decision was wrong. Creating another account to get around a timeout or suspension counts as evasion and leads to both being suspended.
