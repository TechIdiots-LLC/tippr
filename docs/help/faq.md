# Frequently asked questions

## What is {{SITE_NAME}}?

{{SITE_NAME}} is a set of communities, called vaults, where members share links and text posts, comment on them and vote. Posts with more upvotes rise to the top; anyone can start a vault about anything that fits the [content policy](/help/contentpolicy).

## How do vaults work?

Each vault (written /v/name) has its own moderators, rules and look. Subscribe to the ones you like and their posts make up your front page. Before you've subscribed to anything, your front page shows the site's default vaults. Find more on the [vaults page](/vaults/) or with [search](/help/search).

## What do votes and karma do?

Votes decide what rises and falls in a listing. Your karma is roughly the total of the votes on your posts and comments. It isn't a currency and you can't spend it, but some vaults require a little before you can post there, to keep spam out.

## How do I format a post or comment?

Posts and comments use Markdown: see [formatting](/help/formatting).

## What does the AI label on a post mean?

When posting, members say whether their work is human-made, AI-assisted or AI-generated, and the label shows beside the title. Some creative vaults only take human-made and AI-assisted work; others are for AI-generated art and music.

## What are trophies?

Small awards on your profile for things like verifying your email address or being a member for a year. See [trophies](/help/trophies).

## How do I keep my profile out of search engines?

In your [preferences](/prefs/), under privacy options, tick "don't allow search engines to index my user profile". Your profile page then asks search engines not to index it. Most do as asked, but it can take them a while to drop pages they already have, and your posts and comments still appear in the vaults where you made them. Your [privacy settings](/prefs/privacy) also let you download your data or delete your account.

## Why was my post removed?

Moderators remove posts that break their vault's rules, and the spam filter removes posts that look like spam. If you think a post was removed by mistake, message the vault's moderators (the link is in the vault's sidebar). For removals by the site's staff, see [timeouts and suspensions](/help/suspensions).

## I'm being asked to slow down

New accounts and accounts with little karma can only post so often, to keep spam out; the limit eases as you take part. If it's getting in the way, [tell the admins](/contact#ratelimit).

## Is there an app or an API?

The site works in a phone's browser. For programs and bots there's an API: see [using the API](/help/api). Most listings also have [RSS feeds](/help/rss).

## How do I contact the people who run {{SITE_NAME}}?

Use the [contact page](/contact). It sends a message to the admins from your account, and their reply comes back to your inbox.
